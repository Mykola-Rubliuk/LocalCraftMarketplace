# CA2 Group Retrospective

## Retain

Dividing the design into four use-case slices allowed each group member to focus on a specific business workflow. Shared interfaces and regular integration checks helped maintain consistency across the overall design.

## Problem

The slices progressed at different speeds, and some shared architectural decisions were introduced late. This caused inconsistencies between operation contracts, sequence diagrams and the integrated class diagram, requiring additional integration work.

## Specific Improvement

For future projects, the group should agree on shared interfaces, naming conventions and operation signatures before developing individual slices. Each slice should have an assigned reviewer and an intermediate deadline. Regular cross-slice reviews should be conducted throughout the project rather than only before submission.
