# Definition of Done

A design backlog item can move to Done only when:
1. Editable source is committed and readable.
2. Identifiers and terminology match the agreed glossary and baseline.
3. Gherkin, SSD, contracts, CRC/GRASP and sequence agree where applicable.
4. Traceability is updated.
5. A peer review has occurred; comments are resolved or their disposition recorded.
6. Shared models and interfaces remain consistent.
7. Board status and repository evidence links are updated.
8. AI-supported content is disclosed and human decisions are recorded.
